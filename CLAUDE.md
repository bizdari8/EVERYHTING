# Project instructions

Whenever you build, redesign or review a landing page or a personal-branding website, load the `landing-page-master` skill (`.claude/skills/landing-page-master/SKILL.md`) first and follow it, even if it was not asked for by name. Start with its audit of any existing page, then build or repair.

Do not use it for multi-product stores, e-commerce catalogues or large company websites.
